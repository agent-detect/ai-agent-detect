# instinct

Personal assistant that understands your work and priorities, accepts texts or calls, and uses phones and computers to complete tasks. [Official source](https://instinct.com/).

## Identity signals

- non-empty `INSTINCT_OUTER_TOOL_USE_ID` OR non-empty `INSTINCT_OUTER_TOOL_USE_STARTED_AT` OR non-empty `INSTINCT_OUTER_TOOL_USE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS`.

Redacted observation of Instinct command execution in Linux cloud sandboxes (2026-10-31). The three documented names arrived non-empty and were checked by name only. No marker values are published. This is not a vendor-wide injection guarantee or coverage of other interfaces/operating systems.
