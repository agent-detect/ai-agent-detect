# workbuddy

Tencent's AI workspace that plans workflows, uses tools, and creates finished documents, spreadsheets, presentations, and other work. [Official source](https://www.workbuddy.cn/docs/workbuddy/From-Beginner-to-Expert-Guide/Product-Guide).

## Identity signals

- non-empty `WORKBUDDY_APPLICATION_NAME` OR non-empty `WORKBUDDY_APP_NAME`.

Static inspection of WorkBuddy 5.8.3 macOS distribution. Product-name variables are execution signals in that inspected path; their spelling/case is not a matching restriction. Other builds/interfaces were not established.
